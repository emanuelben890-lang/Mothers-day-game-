How to Play Shithead 🃏

The Basics

Get rid of all your cards before the CPU does. Each card must be equal to or higher than the top of the pile.

  • Play your hand first, then your face-up cards, then your face-down cards — those are blind, good luck!
  • Can't play? Hit "Pick up pile" and take the whole pile into your hand
  • You can play the same card on itself (a 6 on a 6, etc.)

Special Cards ✨

  • 2 — Resets the pile, anything can follow
  • 5 — Mirror: copies the card beneath it (if on a 7, next player still plays lower)
  • 7 — Next player must play lower than a 7
  • 8 — Skips the next player, you go again
  • 10 — Clears the pile completely
  • 4 of a kind — Four in a row clears the pile
